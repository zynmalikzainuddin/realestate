HOST PLANT SCIENCE

The root system has a partner.

Sandalwood is a partial root parasite. It connects to a nearby host plant through a specialised structure called a haustorium, drawing selected nutrients and water that help it grow well.

This is why a careful host plant setup is an important part of the cultivation journey. The exact planting specification and care schedule should be confirmed with Pentora. [VERIFY]

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED CALMLY

Good questions belong in the process.

If an answer depends on the agreement, the answer says so. That is part of transparent investing.

What is Haustorium cultivation?+

Sandalwood is a partial root parasite. A haustorium connects to a host plant’s root system and supports nutrient and water exchange. The exact host setup for this project should be confirmed. [VERIFY]

How long does sandalwood take to mature?+

The project’s stated expected maturity is 12-15 years. Actual growth and harvest timing can depend on site, care, approvals and other conditions.

How does the EMI facility work?+

Pentora describes a 0% interest EMI facility. The plot price, tenure, schedule, missed-payment handling and other terms must be read in the final documents. [VERIFY]

Who owns the trees and the plot?+

This depends on the legal structure and agreement. Ask Pentora for the ownership, allocation and participation documents before deciding. [VERIFY]

How is the harvest sold?+

The harvest and sale pathway must follow applicable Kerala permissions and process. Pentora should provide the verified process in writing. [VERIFY]

What security is provided?+

The project brief states 24/7 security and surveillance, including fencing, patrol and CCTV as the monitoring approach. Exact coverage and operating arrangements should be confirmed. [VERIFY]

Can NRIs enquire or participate?+

Please contact Pentora for the eligibility, documentation, payment and compliance requirements applicable to NRI participation. [VERIFY]

What happens if I cannot continue payments?+

Any pause, transfer, refund, forfeiture or exit provisions must be stated in the agreement. Do not rely on verbal assurances. [VERIFY]

Are returns guaranteed?+

No. Sandalwood cultivation involves long-term agricultural and market risks. Returns are not guaranteed. Please read all documents before investing.
